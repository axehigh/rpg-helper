# Intro

The village of Heartstead was once a prosperous trading post, but a week ago a thick, sweet-smelling fog rolled in from the surrounding fens.
The town is now infected with a rotting, putrid plague, yet the villagers are not dying in agony — they are singing and celebrating with ecstatic joy, worshiping a "Generous Grandfather" who has freed them from pain.
A worried merchant whose family still lives in Heartstead has hired you to break through the quarantine, stop the source of this "joyous blight," and save who you can before the plague overflows the valley.

# Objectives

- Investigate the outlying farms of Heartstead and survive the "blessed" welcome.
- Infiltrate the high street of Heartstead and bypass the rotting guards and daemonic imps.
- Confront Abbot Throt inside the desecrated village chapel and silence the defiled Rot-Bell.
- Neutralize the bubbling Cauldron of Poxes and decide the fate of Heartstead.

## Read Aloud

The air grows heavy, carrying a sweet, sickly-sweet aroma like overripe fruit and stagnant pondwater.
Ahead, the outline of Heartstead's outlying farms rises from the fog.
Strange, lively singing echoes through the mist.
As you draw closer, you see a group of villagers dancing hand-in-hand around a cracked cauldron.
But as the fog parts, your breath catches.
Their skin is swollen and green, their clothes stained with dried pus, and bloated blisters cover their arms.
Yet they are smiling, laughing, and beckoning you to join their joyous circle.

## Read Aloud (Norsk)

Luften blir tung og bærer med seg en søtlig, kvalmende duft som overmoden frukt og stillestående vann.
Foran dere stiger konturene av Heartsteads ytterste gårder frem fra tåken.
Merkelig, livlig sang ekkoer gjennom disen.
Etter hvert som dere kommer nærmere, ser dere en gruppe landsbyboere som danser hånd i hånd rundt en sprukken kjele.
Men idet tåken letter, setter dere pusten i halsen.
Huden deres er hoven og grønn, klærne er flekket med tørket verk, og oppblåste blemmer dekker armene deres.
Likevel smiler de, ler, og vinker dere inn i sin gledesfylte sirkel.

## Environment

The outlying farms and country lane leading into Heartstead. Thick fog clings to the wet grass.

## Enemies

- blighted-peasant x4
- marsh-hound x2

## DM Notes

- The Welcoming Committee: 4 blighted peasants and 2 marsh hounds dance in a delirious circle around a cracked wooden cauldron bubbling with stagnant, rancid broth.
- The Infection: The peasants are horribly bloated, rotting, and covered in sores, but their minds are completely numb to the pain, locked in a joyous, maniacal trance.
- Sharing the Blessing: If the heroes refuse to drink the putrid broth, the peasants smile warmly and attack, declaring that they must "share the Grandfather's great gifts!"

## Tactics & Synergy

- Joyous Grab: The blighted peasants shamble forward blindly, ignoring attacks, trying to grapple heroes and pull them closer to spread the Rotting Touch.
- Flesh Exploders: When a peasant drops to 0 HP, they burst into a spray of toxic sludge. Emphasize the threat of Joyous Burst to melee combatants.
- Pack Hunt: The marsh hounds circle the flanks, using Muddy Grip to hold heroes down, then attacking grappled targets with advantage.

## Aftermath & Clues

With the dance halted, one peasant lies gasping, the manic smile fading into tears.
Before passing, they whisper: "The abbot... Throt... tolls the Rot-Bell in the chapel... we must keep dancing so the broth stays sweet..."

# Scene: The High Street of Heartstead

![The High Street of Heartstead](adventures/the-joyous-blight-of-plaguestead/images/02_high_street.jpg)

- id: scene-2
- battlemap: ttb1-p17-l-high-street

## Read Aloud

As you step onto the High Street of Heartstead, the joyous madness of the village becomes a choking nightmare.
Weeping green moss clings to the cobblestones, and a thin, yellowish-green vapor clings to the ground.
In the gutters, tiny, green-skinned creatures — no larger than housecats, with bloated bellies and wide, toothy grins — frolic in the filth, throwing handfuls of rotting muck at each other.
Tearing through the fog come massive, hulking figures clad in rusted, pitted plate armor, their breath rattling through wet, congested iron helms.
They raise rusted halberds, eager to introduce you to their Grandfather's grace.

## Read Aloud (Norsk)

Idet dere går inn på Heartsteads hovedgate, blir landsbyens gledesfylte vanvidd til et kvelende mareritt.
Dryppende, grønn mose klamrer seg til brosteinene, og en tynn, gulgrønn damp ligger lavt over bakken.
I rennesteinen leker bittelåte, grønnhudede skapninger – ikke større enn huskatter, med oppblåste mager og brede, tannete glis – i sølen mens de kaster råtten gjørme på hverandre.
Ut av tåken stormer digre, tunge skikkelser kledd i rusten, tæret platerustning.
Pusten deres rasler gjennom våte, tette jernhjelmer.
De hever rustne hellebarder, ivrige etter å introdusere dere for sin bestefars nåde.

## Environment

The main cobblestone thoroughfare. Thick green moss covers the stones, making it difficult terrain off the direct paths. Toxic yellow-green vapor impairs visibility beyond 40 ft.

## Enemies

- rot-knight x2
- nurgling-swarm x2

## DM Notes

- Nurglings in the Gutters: 2 nurgling swarms are playing in the toxic sludge, cackling and capering. They are aggressive, unpredictable, and highly evasive.
- The Rot Guards: 2 hulking rot knights stand guard in front of the heavy wooden chapel gates, completely devoted to preventing anyone from interrupting the abbot's work.
- Aura of Rust: When a metallic weapon hits a rot knight, it becomes pitted and deals -1 damage until the wielder polishes it during a short or long rest. Remind players of the effect when they strike the knights.
- Past the Guards: If the heroes slip past the knights instead of fighting, the chapel's old iron-banded door can be forced (DC 15 Strength) or picked (DC 13 thieves' tools) — or the key can be lifted from a knight's belt with a clever Sleight of Hand.
- A Moment to Breathe: Once the street is quiet, the fog settles and the village falls eerily still. Offer the party a short rest before the chapel while the nurglings scatter — they'll need it for Throt.

## Tactics & Synergy

- Iron Wall: The rot knights form a solid frontline, blocking the path with their rusted halberds and absorbing hits with their high AC.
- Nurgling Distraction: The nurglings crawl underfoot and leap from gutters, using Poison Filth to poison backliners and Mischievous Dodging to slip away from melee counters.
- Aura Abuse: The knights stay close to heroes using metal shields/armor, enjoying the passive rotting effects of the environment.

## Aftermath & Clues

The rot knights fall, leaving behind heaps of rusted scrap and putrid black blood.
On one of their belts, you find a heavy, rusted brass key engraved with a weeping three-lobed fly, which fits the chapel's iron lock.

# Scene: The Chapel of the Weeping Three

## Read Aloud

The heavy wooden doors of the village chapel creak open, releasing a wave of hot, humid air thick with the smell of boiling fat and wild copper.
Inside, the pews have been pushed aside to make room for a massive, bubbling copper cauldron of green sludge.
Standing over it, stirring with a giant wooden ladle, is Abbot Throt.
Once a holy man, he is now a bloated, weeping mountain of flesh, his eyes twinkling with a manic, grandfatherly affection.
In his other hand, he holds a rope tied to the great brass bell in the rafters.
"Ah, new guests!" he wetly chuckles, pulling the rope.
The bell tolls with a deep, vibrating resonance that makes your wounds itch and burn.

## Read Aloud (Norsk)

De tunge tredørene til landsbykapellet knirker opp og slipper ut en bølge av varm, fuktig luft som er tykk av lukten av kokende fett og villkobber.
Innvendig er kirkebenkene skjøvet til side for å gi plass til en enorm, boblende kobberkjele med grønt slam.
Over den står abbed Throt og rører med en gigantisk tresleiv.
En gang var han en hellig mann, men nå er han et oppblåst, væskende fjell av kjøtt, med øyne som glitrer av en manisk, bestefaderlig kjærlighet.
I den andre hånden holder han et tau knyttet til den store messingklokken i takbjelkene.
"Å, nye gjester!" humrer han vått og drar i tauet.
Klokken klinger med en dyp, vibrerende klang som får sårene deres til å klø og brenne.

## Environment

The desecrated stone interior of the chapel. A massive copper cauldron dominates the sanctuary altar, bubbling with volatile, green plague-broth.

## Enemies

- abbot-throt
- blighted-peasant x4
- nurgling-swarm x2

## DM Notes

- Abbot Throt: Once the protector of Heartstead, now completely corrupted into a fanatical Apostle of Decay. He believes the plague is a beautiful, loving gift.
- The Defiled Rot-Bell: Hanging from the central rafters. Its tolling pulses with necrotic energy, accelerating the plague's maturation and keeping his minions alive.
- Sacrilegious Workshop: The pews, icons, and holy books have been thrown into heaps, covered in green slime and crawling with nurglings.

## Tactics & Synergy

- Pestilent Bell: Abbot Throt uses his Rot-Bell Toll at the start of his turn to either heal his blighted servants or poison the heroes.
- Noxious Guard: Throt releases a Plague Cloud as a bonus action, forcing melee heroes to take poison damage while his blighted peasants form a defensive meat shield.
- Final Stand: Once Throt drops below half HP, he uses Final Bloom to summon 2 more blighted peasants directly from the boiling cauldron.

## Aftermath & Clues

Abbot Throt collapses against the cauldron, his heavy censer-flail clattering to the floor.
As he gasps his last, the great brass bell cracks in two, its tolling silenced.
But the bubbles in the giant copper vat grow frantic, and the cauldron begins to tilt...

# Scene: The Bitter Choice

![The Bitter Choice](adventures/the-joyous-blight-of-plaguestead/images/scene4.jpg)

- id: scene-4

## Read Aloud

The abbot is gone, but his putrid work remains.
The massive copper cauldron is tipping over, its rim bubbling with virulent, boiling rot.
If it overflows and spills into the chapel vaults, it will seep into the village water supply and flow down the fens, spreading Nurgle's joyous blight to the entire valley.
The altars still hold the village's sacred, uncorrupted saintly relics, and the damp timbers of the wooden roof are ripe for flame.
You must decide how to neutralize the Grandfather's gift, and each choice has a bitter price.

## Read Aloud (Norsk)

Abbeden er borte, men hans motbydelige arbeid gjenstår.
Den massive kobberkjelen tipper over, og kanten bobler med virulent, kokende råte.
Hvis den flommer over og lekker ned i kapellhvelvene, vil den sive inn i landsbyens vannforsyning og flyte nedover myrene, og spre Nurgles gledesfylte pest til hele dalen.
Altrene rommer fremdeles landsbyens hellige, uberørte helgenrelikvier, og det fuktige tømmeret i tretaket er klart for flammer.
Dere må velge hvordan dere skal nøytralisere bestefarens gave, og hvert valg har en bitter pris.

## Environment

The high-altar sanctuary. The bubbling cauldron is tilting precariously as acidic green rot begins to crack the floor tiles.

## DM Notes

- The Old Runes: Beneath the sanctuary floor, the village's original crypt is carved with druidic runes older than the settlement itself. A hero with Arcana or Nature recognizes they could be woven to siphon the rot — the key to the Flesh Siphon option.
- Option 1 — The Sacred Sacrifice: Pour the temple's ancient relics of Saint Elspeth directly into the cauldron. The holy magic completely purifies the rot, curing the surviving peasants, but the priceless relics are dissolved and lost forever.
- Option 2 — The Iron Quarantine: Set the wooden rafters of the chapel ablaze and bar the heavy iron gates. The holy fire incinerates the plague-broth, saving the valley, but the chapel, its relics, and any infected seeking sanctuary inside are burned to ash.
- Option 3 — The Flesh Siphon: Use ancient druidic runes to draw the rotting plague directly into the heroes' own life force. This saves the chapel, the relics, and the villagers, but each hero carries a permanent scar of Nurgle (-2 Max HP or a minor rot-scar).

## Aftermath & Clues

The choice is made, and the green fog begins to lift as the morning sun breaks through the fens.
If the heroes saved the relics, the village elders gift them the Saint's Censer (+1 to saving throws against poison and disease).
If they burned the chapel or took the rot into themselves, the heavy-hearted survivors gift them whatever gold and rusted trinkets they could salvage, along with a deep, solemn gratitude for a valley spared from the Grandfather's embrace.
