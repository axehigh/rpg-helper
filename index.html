RPG Helper

Open the DM screen on your laptop and the player screen on the TV or tablet.

Two devices (tablet/TV follows the DM screen)
  1. Run npm start in this folder (or node server.js).
  2. On this device open http://localhost:8080/dm.html#<adventure>.
  3. On the tablet open the http://<this device's IP>:8080 URL printed by the server, e.g. /player.html#<adventure>.
  4. Scene changes on the DM screen push to the tablet automatically.
