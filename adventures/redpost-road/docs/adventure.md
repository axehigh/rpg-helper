# Redpost Road

*A standalone combat scene for 4 players, level 2, lasting 1–1.5 hours.*

---

## The Hook

The road between the waystations runs straight and lonely, marked with red posts
that locals say are painted in blood. The Redpost gang — goblins under a
bugbear leader — has been waylaying wagons for a fortnight. Tonight they have
chosen their ground.

## The Party's Objectives

- Survive the Redpost ambush.
- Pursue the gang back to its camp.
- Put the bugbear leader down for good.

---

## The Storyline

### 1. The Ambush
The party rounds a bend to find an overturned wagon blocking the road — the
lure. As they step past it, goblin skirmishers burst from the ditches, a goblin
archer holds the far hedge, and a worg circles the treeline. The goblins fight
hit-and-run, darting out of cover and back. Once two goblins fall, the rest
retreat down a game trail.

### 2. The Redpost Camp
The trail opens onto the gang's camp: fire pits, a red banner, a heap of
plunder, and the bugbear leader with its goblin hexer. The bugbear holds the
center and fights savagely; the hexer softens the party with curses and smoke.
When the bugbear falls, the gang is finished, and the road is safe again.

### Epilogue
The loot — a merchant's strongbox, two healing potions, and a ledger naming the
gang's fence — is the party's to return or keep.
