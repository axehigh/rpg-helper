# Image Prompts: The Root of All Sorrow

This document contains AI image generation prompts (optimized for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion) to create visual assets for this adventure.

## Aesthetic Guidelines
*   **Style:** Grimdark, high-contrast, hyper-realistic dark fantasy with deep body horror.
*   **Key Theme:** "Patient Despair" — the quiet, courteous horror of a village that has fed a tree for centuries. No joy, no frenzy; everything slow, ancient, and roots.
*   **Color Palette:** Cold greys, bone-white, bruised purple-browns, the deep black-green of swollen apples, grey lichen, and sickly grey sap that pulses like a heartbeat.

## Scene Prompts

### Scene 1: The Black-Apple Road
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene at twilight. A narrow country road narrows between hedgerows heavy with swollen black apples speckled with grey. In the distance a drowned hamlet: a millwheel still turning in a copper-smelling pond. At the foot of a great tree that has split a mass grave open, a boy sits cross-legged drawing a root over and over in the mud, never looking up. From the direction of a distant village, a single bell begins to toll. Heavy grey fen-mist, cold lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 2: The Hollow Feast
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene at dusk in a medieval market square. Long tables are laid under black-apple trees: bowls of polished stone heaped with black apples, grey loaves, pewter cups of vinegar cider. Gaunt villagers in worn clothes stand motionless and polite, and no one has eaten a single bite. At the head of the table, Reeve Mother Ilse stands with a bright ring of grey lichen glowing on her wrist, her face a rehearsed mask of welcome. Behind her a child whispers a single name into the folds of her skirt while an old woman's hand pulls her away. Swept streets, lit windows, patient dread. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 3: The Unanswered Bell
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy interior of a stone chapel. Pews worn smooth by generations of kneeling, floor stones hollowed where boots have stood. A brass bell hangs over the altar and tolls though no rope moves — the sound rising from below, where a stone stair breathes in and out between the floorboards like sleep. At the head of the stair a robed priest stands still, holding a censer that has not been swung in a long time; his eyes are grey, his mouth is open, and something roots inside it. Cold chapel light, dust in the air, deep shadow. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 4: The Nursery of Rooted Things
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene in a root-lined underground gallery. Between the walls, bodies stand half-sunk in packed earth like stones in a field — the sick and the old of a village, bark grown over their legs, hands folded, black sap weeping from their eyes in slow, patient tears. They do not move; they breathe. Among them a woman in a midwife's apron prunes with gardener's shears the roots creeping up their chests, her face very tired. Dripping black sap, breathing walls, a single source of cold light. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 5: The Sap Galleries
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene in flooded underground galleries where grey sap runs upward toward a village in pulses like a heartbeat. The walls are knotted with roots bearing grey lichen rings — the same rings every villager wears, grown out of the stone. Ahead, where the sap pools and the passage narrows, the roots themselves are standing up to meet the viewer, rising into a shambling warden shape. Stagnant pools, difficult terrain of mud and root, grey bioluminescent glow. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 6: The First Root
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene in a flooded chamber lit from below. At its heart, the original wellstone of a village is wrapped in a colossal tree trunk like a fist around a bone, and from the stone a grey heart pulses — older than the village, older than the valley. Before it stands a figure that was once a man, grown into the wood, his face a mask of bark and sorrow, raising one root-woven hand in greeting, not in threat. Deep cold water to the knees, grey light from below, patient ancient presence. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 7: The Cut, the Torch, the Ring
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene of a moment of choice. A tree-man kneels in still water before an exposed grey heart pulsing from a wrapped wellstone. A black-iron knife and a torch rest on the stone; a ring of grey lichen lies coiled in the hollow where the kneeling figure's hand was, like a sleeping animal. The sap has stopped running; the chamber is silent and still, lit from below by the heart's slow pulse. Weight, silence, consequence. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.
