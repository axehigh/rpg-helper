# The Tolling Bell

*A standalone scene of combat and a small puzzle for 4 players, level 3, lasting 1.5–2 hours.*

---

## The Hook

A half-flooded chapel stands on the marsh road. At dusk its sunken bell tolls
of its own accord, and the mire answers. The locals ring twice and ride on —
but tonight the storm closes in, and the party needs shelter.

## The Party's Objectives

- Take shelter in the flooded chapel before dark.
- Still the tolling bell.
- Leave the mire before the water rises.

---

## The Storyline

### 1. The Flooded Nave
The chapel's nave is awash with black water. As dusk falls the bell rings once
on its own, and the water ripples toward the door. Will-o'-wisps drift in
through the broken windows, and marsh hounds slip in through flooded gaps. The
nave can be held from the dry choir loft, but the fight only ends when the bell
is stilled.

### 2. The Crypt
Beneath the altar, a flooded stair drops into the crypt, where a great drowned
bell lies in a stone basin, tolling through a rusted gear mechanism. Bark-touched
figures stand like mourners — they do not attack while the bell rings, and they
want the knell to continue. The bell can be silenced three ways: chain the
clapper, jam the gears, or cut the rope above. Still the knell and the mourners
sink; leave it ringing and the tolling follows the party up the stairs.

### Epilogue
With the bell still, the crypt's reliquary yields a bell-ringer's hammer (+1
mace), a coffer of 40 gp, and a brass reliquary the village will be glad to see
returned. The chapel is silent for the first time in years.
