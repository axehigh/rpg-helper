# The Iron Shackle Mines

*A tabletop RPG adventure for 4 players, level 2, lasting 3 hours.*

---

## The Hook

A young woman from the frontier outpost of Rockfall Valley begs the party for help. Her brother and several other townspeople were abducted in the night by the **Iron Shackle** cartel—a brutal gang of slavers who force captives to toil in a nearby, supposedly abandoned iron mine. The cartel sells their quarry and the strongest captives to dark markets under the mountains. The party has only a few hours to track them down before a subterranean transport caravan arrives to take the captives deep underground, where they will be lost forever.

## The Party's Objectives

- Track the raiders to the canyon of the Iron Shackle Mines.
- Infiltrate the mining camp on the surface.
- Breach the subterranean slave pens and iron quarries.
- Defeat Taskmaster Gruul and free the captive miners before they are taken deep underground.

---

## The Storyline

### 1. The Canyon Switchback
The party tracks the heavy wagon tracks and dragging footprints of the captives into a dusty, red-rock canyon. The trail climbs along a narrow, winding switchback. The cartel has posted a guard post at a stone bridge spanning a deep chasm. Slaver scouts and their vicious **Slaver Hounds** lie in wait, ready to roll boulders onto intruders or ambush them from above.
*Battlemap: ttlb-p35-l-bridge / ttlb-p36-r-bridge*

### 2. The Surface Mine Camp
The entrance to the mines is surrounded by a wooden stockade, watchtowers, and makeshift tents. Slaver thugs guard the perimeter, drinking and gambling. The gates are heavily barred, but a loose timber in the palisade or a sneak attack on the lone watchtower sentry offers a way inside. Inside, the camp is a clutter of ore carts, mining crates, and timber piles, allowing for stealth and tactical maneuvers.
*Battlemap: ttb1-p7-l-village-walls / ttb1-p8-r-village-walls*

### 3. The Quarry and Slave Pens
An iron grate in the cliffside leads into the dark, dusty iron mines. Inside, the sound of rhythmic pickaxes and cracking whips echoes through the tunnels. The party discovers slave pens built into the raw stone, where the shivering captives are kept in iron cages between grueling shifts. Tough enforcers patrol the tunnels, keeping the prisoners submissive. The party must defeat the guards to retrieve the cage keys and free the miners.
*Battlemap: ttlb-p27-l-cellar / ttlb-p28-r-tunnel*

### 4. The Taskmaster's Forge
The deepest chamber is a subterranean forge where iron ore is smelted and heavy shackles are hammered out. At the center of the hall, the cartel leader, **Taskmaster Gruul**, is preparing to pack the captives into a heavy iron cage-wagon. Gruul is a massive, scarred bugbear who wields a heavy iron mallet and a chain whip. Surrounded by his loyal guards, he will fight to the death to protect his lucrative operation.
*Battlemap: ttb1-p31-l-underground / ttb1-p32-r-underground*

---

## Epilogue

With Taskmaster Gruul defeated, the surviving captives are released from their heavy chains. Hidden in the taskmaster's iron desk, the party finds a heavy iron chest. Inside is the cartel's gold stash, a magical **Ring of the Ram** (used by Gruul to shatter rocks and disobedient miners), and a trade ledger detailing the Iron Shackle cartel's mysterious wealthy backer: a high-ranking noble in the capital. The grateful people of Rockfall Valley reward the heroes with their modest gold savings and a lifetime of hospitality.
