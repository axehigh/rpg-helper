# Image Prompts: The Joyous Blight of Heartstead

This document contains AI image generation prompts (optimized for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion) to create visual assets for this adventure.

## Aesthetic Guidelines
*   **Style:** Grimdark, high-contrast, hyper-realistic dark fantasy.
*   **Key Theme:** "Joyous Horror" — the contrast between grotesque decay and blissful, manic happiness.
*   **Color Palette:** Sickly greens, rusted oranges, deep greys, and volatile yellows.

## Scene Prompts

### Scene 1: The Singing Outpost
**Prompt:** A grimdark fantasy scene at twilight. In a misty, low-lit country lane outside a dilapidated medieval farm, a group of 4 bloated, sickly-green-skinned peasants are dancing hand-in-hand in a joyful, manic circle. They wear tattered, pus-stained burlap rags. Their faces are stretched into wide, ecstatic, unblinking grins despite their weeping sores and bloated blisters. In the center of their circle is a large, cracked wooden cauldron bubbling with a thick, glowing neon-green broth. A sickly green vapor rises from the cauldron, mixing with the heavy grey fog. The atmosphere is eerie and unsettling, contrasting the grotesque decay of the people with their bizarre, blissful happiness. Cinematic lighting, hyper-realistic textures, 8k, dark fantasy art style.

### Scene 2: The High Street of Heartstead
**Prompt:** A narrow medieval cobblestone high street choked with thick, weeping green moss and a low-lying yellow toxic vapor. In the foreground, dozens of tiny, mischievous green imps (Nurglings) with bloated bellies and wide toothy grins are frolicking in the gutters, throwing handfuls of rotting filth. In the midground, two hulking knights in heavily rusted, pitted green-tinged plate armor stand guard, their iron helms weeping a thick black ichor. The buildings are crooked and decaying, with green fungus growing from the timber frames. Grimdark, atmospheric lighting, hyper-realistic, 8k, cinematic horror.

### Scene 3: The Chapel of the Weeping Three
**Prompt:** Inside a desecrated Gothic stone chapel. The air is thick with a humid, yellowish haze. In the center sanctuary, a massive, bubbling copper cauldron overflows with glowing green sludge. Standing over it is a monstrously bloated, weeping priest (Abbot Throt) in tattered, filthy religious robes, his face twisted into a manic, loving smile. He holds a giant wooden ladle in one hand and a rope tied to a massive, cracked brass bell in the other. Tiny grinning imps crawl over the altar and the boiling vat. Dramatic copper and green lighting, high contrast, hyper-detailed, dark fantasy oil painting style.

### Scene 4: The Bitter Choice
**Prompt:** A cinematic close-up of a massive copper cauldron in a stone chapel sanctuary, tilted precariously and about to overflow with acidic, bubbling green rot. To one side, a pile of ancient, glowing gold and silver saintly relics sits on a stone altar, untouched by decay. To the other side, the damp, rotting wooden rafters of the chapel roof are visible above. The lighting is a clash of divine golden light from the relics and a sickly, volatile green glow from the cauldron. High stakes, intense atmosphere, realistic textures, 8k.

## Monster Prompts

### The Nurgling Swarms
**Prompt:** A cluster of dozens of tiny, green-skinned daemonic imps, each about the size of a housecat. They have disproportionately large, bloated bellies, spindly limbs, and faces dominated by wide, ecstatic grins and needle-like teeth. They are piled on top of each other in a writhing, giggling heap, covered in slime and filth. White background, concept art style, hyper-detailed, grotesque but whimsical.

### The Rot Knights
**Prompt:** A full-body concept art of a hulking warrior in ancient, pitted plate armor that is covered in thick orange rust and patches of green moss. A sickly green vapor leaks from the joints of the armor. The knight carries a massive, rusted halberd. The helmet’s visor reveals only a single, wet, milky-white eye. The pose is slow and menacing. Grimdark, hyper-realistic, weathered textures, 8k.

### The Marsh Hounds
**Prompt:** Full-body concept art of a swamp-hunting hound warped by the Grandfather's blessing. Its lanky frame is bloated with sickly green-grey flesh, mottled with weeping sores and patches of green moss clinging to matted, rotting fur. Thick brackish sludge drips from its jowls, and its mouth hangs in a wide, manic, ecstatic grin full of crooked, yellowed teeth. Its eyes are glazed with a blissful, feverish warmth. Waterlogged reeds are tangled in its coat, and a faint yellow-green vapor rises from its sodden body, leaving a trail of sucking mud behind it. Grimdark, hyper-realistic, weathered textures, 8k.

### Abbot Throt, Apostle of Decay
**Prompt:** Full-body concept art of a monstrously obese priest. His skin is a pale, sickly green, stretched thin over his massive frame and weeping with sores. He wears tattered, once-regal religious vestments stained with black ichor. In one hand, he wields a massive, smoking censer-flail made of rusted iron; in the other, a giant, filth-covered wooden ladle. His expression is one of blissful, fatherly warmth and madness. Dark fantasy art, hyper-detailed, 8k.
