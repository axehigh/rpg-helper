# Shadows of Blackstone Keep

*A tabletop RPG adventure for 4 players, level 3, lasting 3–4 hours.*

---

## The Hook

The party arrives at the edge of Oakhollow as the village burns. Smoke chokes the
sky, throwing the sunset into crimson shadows. Houses crackle in flames while
villagers flee down the dirt roads. Between the burning storefronts, goblins dart
— a raiding party, organized and quick.

## The Party's Objectives

- Save the remaining villagers and douse critical fires.
- Drive off or eliminate the tactical raiders.
- Rescue Elder Hadrick, or recover the map dropped in the chaos.

---

## The Storyline

### 1. The Oakhollow Raiders
The party throws back the goblin raid on the burning village square. It quickly
becomes clear the raiders are no random mob — they looted specific storehouses
and took **Elder Hadrick**, the village blacksmith, who carries an ancient
**dwarven key**. As the raiders fall, a crude map drops, marking a path deep into
the Blackwood Forest, and a wounded ranger stumbles forward with a warning:
*"They dragged Elder Hadrick away... Kragath has already entered the valley. If he
reaches the Heartforge with that key, we're finished."*

### 2. Through Blackwood Forest
The party tracks the warband deeper into the woods, following fresh bootprints and
claw marks. The trail tells its own story: a merchant cart shattered not by axes
but by crushing jaws — a glimpse of the Worgs yet to come; a vanguard campsite
where goblins slept in the mud while hobgoblins took the dry ground, revealing the
harsh hierarchy of Kragath's army. A shortcut through a ravine lies blocked by
giant spider webs, forcing a choice between a quiet detour and a longer march
against the fading daylight.

### 3. Dwarven Milestones
Deep in the forest stand ancient, mossy monoliths carved with dwarven runes.
Deciphered, they read: **"Beware the Heartforge's light; it warms the just, but
burns the greedy."** — a dark foreshadowing of what waits in the keep. Reading the
runes draws out a warband lying in ambush.

### 4. Blackstone Keep
The trail climbs through jagged peaks to the edge of a deep, misty gorge. Built
into the sheer mountain face stands **Blackstone Keep**, an ancient and forgotten
dwarven stronghold — its great central forge, once a hub of roaring industry, now
silent and dead. A magnificent but fractured stone bridge arches across the chasm
toward the iron gates, narrowed by collapse into a hazardous bottleneck. The
hobgoblin blockade holds the crossing. When the defenders fall, the party claims
healing potions and pushes on.

### 5. The Collapsed Hall
Beyond the heavy iron doors lies a grand, dusty hall lined with monumental dwarven
statues. The floor is coated in a thick, unnatural layer of white dust left by
Kragath's vanguard. Among the rubble, a crushed goblin corpse is pinned beneath
fallen masonry — tucked into its armor, a **healing potion and a parchment** that
holds the key to waking the Heartforge:
*"First, blast the coals with wind. Second, heat the coals with fire. Third, shape
the heavy metal. Fourth, cool it in the deep waters."*

### 6. The Forge Locks
The way forward is sealed by towering iron doors etched with massive gears. Four
ancient dwarven stone wheels, each carved with a glowing rune, are built into the
wall — the locks of the master smith's forge. Aligned in the correct forging order
(Wind, Fire, Iron, Water), the doors yield. But the grinding of the mechanism
echoes through the dark, drawing goblin skirmishers from a hidden panel while the
party works the wheels.

### 7. The Heartforge
The final chamber: a massive circular hall where channels of molten lava surround
a central platform, reached only by narrow, broken iron bridges and swinging
chains. Deep beneath the floor grates the ancient Heartforge pulses with an angry,
blinding orange light — and at the central altar stands **Kragath Iron Fang**,
holding the dwarven key as the machinery awakens.

The battle is waged across shaking bridges and erupting steam. When Kragath falls,
his body crushes the key inside the control mechanism, and the Heartforge turns
volatile. The party must escape within five rounds as lava channels overflow and
the towers collapse behind them — bursting through the outer gates just as the
keep comes down.

### Epilogue
The sky clears and the sun sets peacefully over a saved valley. Elder Hadrick,
released from his captivity, presents the heroes their due rewards, and the
dwarven forge spirits bless each of them with a small magical gift — a +1 weapon,
a protective charm, or a glowing heirloom shield.
